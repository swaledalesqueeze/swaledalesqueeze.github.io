# Swaledale Squeeze @ Nightingale Centre

## 21-23 May 2027


The Swaledale Squeeze is a convivial weekend of concertina playing, meeting friends, a concert, a ceilidh, sessions. From 2027 we are moving to a new venue, the Nightingale Centre in Derbyshire, because Grinton Lodge has closed. The [Nightingale Centre](https://independenthostels.co.uk/members/nightingalecentre/) is based just in Great Hucklow, just outside Buxton in the Derbyshire Dales.

<div style="text-align:center"><img src="assets/images/ssicon.png" alt="Squeeze logo"></div>

The guest tutors for the 2027 squeeze are

- Lorraine Ni Bhriain on Anglo
- Alex Wade on English
- TBA on Duet

Our additional tutors are Harry Scurfield (Anglo), Paul Walker (English/Anglo), Carolyn Wade (Band) Steven Bradley (Accompanied singing) and Dave Ball (English). Together they provide workshops covering a whole range of concertina playing for different abilities, systems and styles. Dave Elliott is joining on Saturday for any instrument repairs. Chris Algar is coming on Sunday for concertina purchases.

<div style="text-align:center"><img src="assets/images/ssicon.png" alt="Squeeze logo"></div>

The weekend starts on Friday evening with informal sessions including the Dotty session, with written music provided for the tunes. Saturday includes teaching workshops in the morning and afternoon, an informal tea-time concert and a ceilidh in the evening, with the band for the ceilidh and the 'spots' provided by participants. There are more workshops on Sunday morning before the farewell concert on Sunday afternoon featuring spots from the tutors and their workshop groups.

<div style="text-align:center"><img src="assets/images/ssicon.png" alt="Squeeze logo"></div>

All of the workshop sessions take place in the Centre. Coffee and tea are provided in break times. Alcoholic drinks are available to buy from the Centre and you are welcome to bring your own alcohol, subject to a £5 donation to the Centre.

<div style="text-align:center"><img src="assets/images/ssicon.png" alt="Squeeze logo"></div>

Most people stay in the Centre itself, in comfortable rooms with standard beds with provided bedding and towels. There are a limited number of single rooms. All rooms are up a flight of stairs with the exception of one accessible room.

 Meals (breakfast, lunch and dinner) are included for all those staying in the Centre, and as en extra if required for people staying in tents, campervans or off-site, from Friday evening to Sunday lunchtime.

<div style="text-align:center"><img src="assets/images/ssicon.png" alt="Squeeze logo"></div>

In the grounds of the Centre there is room for several campervans (on hard standing) and tents.


There is a small self-catering kitchen in the Centre, with stove microwave, fridge but no cooking utensils, and can access showers and toilets in the hostel.

<div style="text-align:center"><img src="assets/images/ssicon.png" alt="Squeeze logo"></div>

Non residents are welcome to participate in the full programme and to order meals if required.

Dogs on leads are permitted everywhere apart from the dining rooms and workshops. Make sure they are kept on a lead outside as there are farm animals nearby.

<div style="text-align:center"><img src="assets/images/ssicon.png" alt="Squeeze logo"></div>

You can find out more about ticket prices and book through [EventBee](https://swaledale-squeeze-2027.eventbee.com) from 6th December 2026.
